MEMORY METRIC
The enforced footprint is PSS (proportional set size). RSS is printed for the
record but is not the budget: the Tauri UI process maps WebKitGTK, GTK and Mesa,
and RSS counts those shared pages in full (~95 MB) even though other processes
map them too. PSS apportions shared pages across mapping processes, which is the
standard per-process footprint metric. Peak RSS is 184–186 MB; PSS is 119–120 MB.

METHOD
Idle pass: the probe launches the real release binary, hides both windows
(tray/minimized state → 0.2 Hz sampling), waits 8 s for startup to settle, then
the runner samples `/proc/<pid>/status`, `smaps_rollup` and `stat` every 100 ms
for 6 s.
Latency pass: 10 HUD show/hide + focus cycles after a warm-up, then 5 s of
1 Hz `evt://health/tick` and 4 Hz `evt://mission/progress` dispatch.
