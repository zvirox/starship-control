Requirement: desktop notification (portal `Notification` via `ashpd`) on: approval needed, mission finished, mission failed, stalled > 2 min. Rate-limit and dedupe. Verify: with Cockpit hidden, completing a mock mission raises exactly one notification.

1. Implementation:
- Enabled `ashpd` feature `notification` in `app/src-tauri/Cargo.toml`.
- Built `NotificationManager` in `app/src-tauri/src/notification.rs`:
  - Triggers supported:
    - `ApprovalNeeded`: once per (mission_id, step_id), cooldown 60s.
    - `MissionFinished`: exactly once per mission_id.
    - `MissionFailed`: exactly once per mission_id.
    - `MissionStalled`: allowed only if stall_seconds >= 120 (2 min), cooldown 300s (5 min).
  - Global rate limiter: at most 1 notification every 1000ms.
  - Portal delivery via `ashpd::desktop::notification::NotificationProxy` with a non-blocking timeout of 150ms to ensure headless/CI/daemon-less systems never hang.
  - Registered command `cmd_dispatch_notification` in `commands.rs` and `main.rs`.
  - Type definitions added in `app/src/types/ipc.ts` (`NotificationTrigger`, `DispatchNotificationPayload`).
